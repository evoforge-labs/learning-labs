# Success Metric

A success metric tells you whether the feature changed user behavior or business outcome.

Bad metric:

- “Feature launched.”
- “Button exists.”
- “Number of lines of code.”

Better metric:

- “Time to complete weekly report drops from 30 minutes to under 5 minutes.”
- “Activation rate increases from 25% to 35%.”
- “Support tickets for setup decrease by 30%.”

A click count can be useful as a leading indicator, but it is rarely the final outcome.

## Rule of thumb

Output answers: “Did we ship it?”

Outcome answers: “Did user behavior or business result improve?”
